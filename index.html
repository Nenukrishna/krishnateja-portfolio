What I do

Built for the
performance brief

Not an editor who gets handed footage. I take a growth problem and return a finished, on-brand master that a media buyer can put straight into a campaign.

01

Performance ad creatives

Meta and Google video built to a brief and a number — installs, signups, CPI. Hook, problem, proof, CTA, cut to the second.

02

Hook-first storytelling

The first 1.5 seconds decide everything. I write and cut openings as pattern interrupts — news desks, interrogations, confessions, absurd devices.

03

AI-assisted production

Full films shot without a crew — generated performances, lipsync, voice, B-roll — then treated as raw footage and cut like live action.

04

Sound design & mix

Dialogue-led mixes with music beds that duck properly, delivered to broadcast loudness so nothing clips in the feed.

05

Motion graphics & VFX

After Effects supers, app UI composites, score meters, badges and end cards — built to drop cleanly over a locked cut.

06

Multi-language delivery

One master, seven Indian languages. Dubbing, subtitle passes and pronunciation QC so every regional cut holds up.
